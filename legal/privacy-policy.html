Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026 Your data stays with us

Royal Style Interiors respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you contact us or use this website, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

In short: we only collect the details you give us to answer your enquiry and deliver your project, and we never sell your personal information.

What we collect & why

When you contact us we may collect your name and contact details, project details (location, type of work, sizes, requirements), any messages and photos you share, and basic technical data such as pages viewed and device type. We use this only to reply to enquiries, prepare quotations, plan and deliver your project, schedule visits and installations, and send project updates. We send promotional messages only with your permission, and you can opt out at any time.

Sharing & retention

We never sell or rent your information. We share it only with trusted providers (hosting, email or messaging tools), team members, vendors or installers involved in your project, or authorities where required by law. We keep it only as long as needed to serve you and meet legal, warranty and accounting requirements, then delete or securely dispose of it.

Your rights & data security

You may ask us at any time to show the information we hold about you, correct inaccurate details, delete it where we are not required to keep it, or stop marketing messages. We take reasonable steps to keep your data safe and limit access, though no internet transmission is completely secure. Our services are for adults, and we do not knowingly collect children's information.
