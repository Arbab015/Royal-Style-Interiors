Crafting luxury interiors with 20 years of Dubai experience

Royal Style Interiors is a luxury carpentry and interior company, headquartered in Islamabad. We bring two decades of experience from Dubai's demanding hotel and luxury housing market to homes, offices and commercial spaces across Pakistan.

Everything we install is designed and manufactured in our own workshop, so we control the quality of every door, wardrobe and wall panel from the first sketch to the final fitting. One team handles your whole project, which keeps the timeline, the budget and the finish under control.

Whether it is a single wardrobe or a complete hotel interior, we treat every project with the same care for detail.

  • Specialists in hotels and luxury houses.
  • Own workshop, so design, manufacturing and installation stay under one roof.
  • Complete solutions: wooden doors, wardrobes, flooring, wall paneling, ceilings, media walls, American kitchens, reception tables and full office and shop interiors.

Our head office is in I-10/4, Islamabad, and our new Abbottabad branch on Mandian Mansehra Road is opening soon. Visit us, call us, or send a message and we will help you plan your space.

Services

Featured Services

Complete Office Work

Complete office woodwork and interior finishing, including custom furniture, storage solutions, workstations, wall panels, and other elements designed to create a functional and professional workspace.

Wooden Doors

Custom-designed and professionally crafted wooden doors made with quality materials, combining durability, elegant designs, and a refined finish to complement residential and commercial interiors.

Wardrobe

Customized wardrobes designed according to available space and storage requirements, featuring practical layouts, quality woodwork, modern designs, and a clean, durable finish.

Wooden Flooring

Professional wooden flooring installation using carefully selected materials and finishes to provide a warm, elegant, and durable appearance while enhancing the overall character of the interior.

Media Wall

Custom media walls designed to create an attractive focal point for living and entertainment areas, incorporating TV placement, storage, decorative panels, and modern design elements.

Hotel Reception Table & Interior

Complete hotel reception woodwork and interior design, including professionally crafted reception counters, wall features, decorative elements, and functional details that create a welcoming and sophisticated environment.

American Kitchen

Custom American-style kitchens designed for modern living, featuring practical storage, spacious countertops, quality cabinetry, and efficient layouts that combine functionality with contemporary aesthetics.

Wooden Ceiling

Custom wooden ceiling work designed to add warmth, texture, and architectural character to interiors, with precise craftsmanship and finishing that complements the overall design.

Shop Interior

Complete shop interior work focused on creating an attractive, functional, and customer-friendly commercial space, including display units, counters, wall treatments, storage, and customized woodwork.
