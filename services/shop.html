Complete shop interiors

A good shop interior helps customers find what they need and helps your staff work. We plan display units, counters and storage together so the whole space feels organised and inviting.

Wall treatments and customized woodwork carry your brand through the shop, and everything is built and installed by one team.

We work on shops of different sizes, from a small outlet to a larger store.

What is included

  • Display units and shelving
  • Counters and cash desks
  • Wall treatments and decorative panels
  • Storage for stock
  • Customized woodwork for your brand
  • Matching finishes across the whole shop
  • Made and fitted by our own team

Why choose Royal Style Interiors

Our own workshop Every unit and counter is made by us, so quality is controlled at every step.
20 years of experience Our standards come from Dubai's demanding commercial and hotel market.
Made to fit Everything is built to your shop's measurements and the products you sell.
One accountable team We handle design, manufacturing and installation, so you deal with one company.

How we work

  1. Consultation and site visitWe discuss your business and products, visit the shop and take measurements.
  2. Design and planningWe plan the layout, displays and counters so customers move easily through the shop.
  3. Materials and finishesWe choose materials and finishes that fit your brand.
  4. ManufacturingEverything is made in our own workshop, where we check the quality.
  5. Installation and handoverOur team installs and finishes the shop interior and hands over a clean space.
