Complete office woodwork and interior finishing

We handle the full woodwork and interior finishing of your office, from the first measurement on site to the final fitting. Every piece is made to suit your layout, your team size and the way your business actually works.

Because design, manufacturing and installation stay under one roof, the finish is consistent across the whole workspace and the timeline stays under control.

Whether you are setting up a new office, moving to a bigger space or refreshing the one you have, we plan the work around your layout and your budget. It suits corporate offices, growing businesses, commercial plazas and professional firms.

What is included

  • Custom workstations, executive desks and meeting tables
  • Executive cabins, cabinets and back-wall units
  • Storage units, shelving and filing cabinets
  • Reception counters and waiting areas
  • Wall panels and partitions
  • Matching finishes across the whole office
  • One team from design to installation

How we work

  1. Consultation and site visitWe listen to what you need, visit the site and take exact measurements.
  2. Design and planningWe prepare the layout and design and adjust it with you until it suits the way you work.
  3. Materials and finishesWe agree materials, colours and finishes together so the whole office looks consistent.
  4. ManufacturingEverything is made in our own workshop, where we check the quality before it leaves for site.
  5. Installation and handoverOur team fits and finishes the work on site and hands over a clean, ready-to-use office.

Planning your office? It helps to know the size of the space or a floor plan, how many people will work there, the rooms you need, your preferred style and your budget. Not sure about some of these? Just call us and we will help you work it out.
