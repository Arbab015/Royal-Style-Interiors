Wooden flooring installed with care

A wooden floor changes the feel of a whole room. We select materials and finishes that suit your space and install them carefully, so the surface is even, tight and long lasting.

Our team prepares the surface, lays the floor and finishes the edges, so the result looks clean from wall to wall.

We install wooden flooring in homes, offices, shops and hotels, in a single room or across a whole property.

What is included

  • Carefully selected materials and finishes
  • Surface preparation before installation
  • Flooring for rooms, offices and shops
  • Neat edges, joints and skirting
  • Clean finishing at doors and corners
  • A warm, elegant and durable appearance
  • Installed by our own team

How we work

  1. Consultation and site visitWe visit the space, discuss what you want and measure the area.
  2. Material and finish selectionWe help you choose the material, colour and finish that suit the room.
  3. Surface preparationWe prepare the surface so the floor sits evenly.
  4. InstallationOur team lays the floor carefully, with tight joints and neat edges.
  5. Finishing and handoverWe finish the edges and details and leave the space clean.
