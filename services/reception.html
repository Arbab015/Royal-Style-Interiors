Hotel reception woodwork and interiors

The reception is the first thing a guest sees. We design and build reception counters, wall features and decorative elements that set the tone for your hotel the moment guests walk in.

Our experience comes from Dubai's hotel and luxury housing market, and we bring the same standards to hotels and commercial buildings across Pakistan.

Each reception is both striking and practical, with the working details that front-desk staff need every day.

What is included

  • Custom reception counters and tables
  • Wall features and decorative panels
  • Functional details for front-desk work
  • Lobby and waiting area woodwork
  • Matching finishes across the reception area
  • Designs to suit your hotel's style and brand
  • Made and fitted by our own team

Why choose Royal Style Interiors

Our own workshop Every counter and panel is made by us, so quality is controlled at every step.
20 years of experience We learned our standards in Dubai's demanding hotel market.
Made to fit The reception is built to your lobby's measurements and layout.
One accountable team Design, manufacturing and installation are handled by one team, so you deal with one company.

How we work

  1. Consultation and site visitWe discuss your hotel's style and needs, visit the lobby and take measurements.
  2. Design and planningWe prepare the design of the counter and walls and agree it with you.
  3. Materials and finishesWe choose materials and finishes that suit your hotel's look.
  4. ManufacturingEverything is made in our own workshop, where we check the quality.
  5. Installation and handoverOur team installs and finishes the reception and hands it over clean and ready.

Planning a hotel reception? It helps to know the size of the lobby, a floor plan if you have one, the style of your hotel and your budget. Not sure? Just call us and we will help you plan it.
