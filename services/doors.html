Custom wooden doors, crafted in our own workshop

A door is one of the first things people notice and touch every day. We design and craft wooden doors that suit your interior, from the design and the finish to the frame and fittings, so they look right and last.

Every door is made in our own workshop from quality materials and measured to your openings, which means a proper fit and a clean finish when it is installed.

We make doors for homes, offices, hotels and commercial buildings, whether you need a single door or a complete set for the whole property.

What is included

  • Main entrance doors
  • Bedroom, bathroom and room doors
  • Office and cabin doors
  • Doors for hotels and commercial buildings
  • Door frames and matching fittings
  • A choice of designs, veneers and finishes
  • Measured, made and fitted by our own team

How we work

  1. Consultation and measurementWe discuss where the doors go and the style you want, then measure each opening.
  2. Design and styleWe agree the design, size and finish of each door with you.
  3. Materials and finishesWe choose the materials, colour and finish so the doors match the rest of your interior.
  4. ManufacturingYour doors and frames are made in our own workshop, where we check the quality.
  5. Installation and finishingWe fit the doors and frames, adjust them and check that every door opens and closes smoothly.

Planning new doors? It helps to know how many doors you need, where they go, the size of the openings and the style and finish you prefer. Not sure? Just call us and we will help you choose.
