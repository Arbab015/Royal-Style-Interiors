Wooden ceilings with precise craftsmanship

A wooden ceiling gives a room warmth and depth that paint cannot. We design the pattern and structure to complement your interior, then build and fit it with precise craftsmanship.

It can be a simple panelled finish or a more detailed feature ceiling, planned with space for lights and fittings.

We work on ceilings for homes, hotels, offices and commercial spaces, matching the design to your doors, walls and furniture.

What is included

  • Panelled and feature ceiling designs
  • Space planned for lights and fittings
  • Precise joints and clean finishing
  • A match to your doors, walls and furniture
  • Full ceilings or selected feature areas
  • Warm, textured finishes
  • Made and fitted by our own team

How we work

  1. Consultation and site visitWe visit the space, discuss the look you want and measure the ceiling.
  2. Design and planningWe prepare the pattern and layout, including space for lights, and agree it with you.
  3. Materials and finishesWe choose the wood and finish that suit your interior.
  4. ManufacturingThe ceiling is made in our own workshop, where we check the quality.
  5. Installation and handoverOur team fits and finishes the ceiling and leaves the space clean.

Thinking of a wooden ceiling? It helps to know the size of the room, the ceiling height, where lights should go and the style you like. Not sure? Just call us and we will help you plan it.
