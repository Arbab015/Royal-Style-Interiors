Custom wardrobes made to fit your space

A good wardrobe makes a room feel organised. We plan each wardrobe around the space available and the way you store your things, so you get a practical layout instead of wasted corners.

Wardrobes are built in our own workshop with quality woodwork and finished to a clean, durable standard, then fitted neatly on site.

Whether it is a single bedroom wardrobe or a full dressing area, we design it to match the style of your home.

What is included

  • Made-to-measure wardrobes for any room
  • Hanging space, shelves and drawers planned for you
  • Sliding or hinged door options
  • Corner and full-wall wardrobes
  • Dressing areas and walk-in wardrobes
  • Modern designs with a durable finish
  • Fitted on site by our own installers

Why choose Royal Style Interiors

Made to fit Each wardrobe is built to your room's measurements, so it uses the space fully.

How we work

  1. Consultation and measurementWe discuss what you need to store and measure the room.
  2. Layout and designWe plan the inside layout and the design of the doors and agree it with you.
  3. Materials and finishesWe choose the materials, colours and handles that suit your room.
  4. ManufacturingYour wardrobe is made in our own workshop, where we check the quality.
  5. Installation and handoverWe fit the wardrobe, adjust the doors and leave your room clean.

Planning a wardrobe? It helps to know the size of the wall or room, what you need to store, the style and colour you like and your budget. Not sure? Just call us and we will help you plan it.
