American-style kitchens built for modern living

We design American-style kitchens around how you cook and live: generous storage, spacious countertops and an efficient layout between the sink, stove and fridge.

Cabinetry is built in our workshop and fitted on site, so doors, drawers and panels line up cleanly.

What is included

  • Quality cabinetry made to your measurements
  • Spacious countertops and practical storage
  • Efficient, well-planned layouts
  • Open-plan and island options
  • Contemporary finishes

How we work

  1. Consultation and measurementWe visit the site, listen to what you need and take exact measurements.
  2. DesignWe prepare the design and agree materials and finishes with you.
